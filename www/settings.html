♥ Remember-a-Day Cherish every moment & milestone · Every date has a story. Remember-a-Day is a personal occasion, memory, and celebration companion designed to help you remember the people, milestones, and everyday moments that matter most—all in one calm, personal space.

Occasions & Countdowns: Organise Birthdays, Anniversaries, Trips, Holidays, Milestones, Festivals, and Special Occasions with automatic yearly rollovers, live day countdowns, search, category filters, and reminder preferences.

Memories & Timeline: Capture memories with notes and photos, look back at past-year moments in On This Day, travel through your story on the Memory Timeline, and inspect photos up close.

Daily Glance & Calendar: See what is happening today, spot special observances, browse upcoming reminders, and tap calendar dates to view keepsake day cards.

Surprise Studio: Turn any birthday, anniversary, festival, or milestone into a personalised greeting card or gift box.

Local-First & Personalised: Saved directly on your device with JSON backup export and restore, plus light/dark themes, accent colours, text size, and compact spacing controls.
